Augmented reality in online retailing: systematic review

In One Line A single map of what drives and results from AR use in online shopping.
International Journal of Retail & Distribution Management (2022) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/IJRDM-06-2021-0287 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"Reviewing 53 articles, the study proposes a framework of consumer behaviour toward AR in online retail and five research priorities: AR's social side, its dark side, customer engagement, experiential value and AR marketing."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, International Journal of Retail & Distribution Management (2022)

Plain-English Summary

AR drew huge attention in online retail, but the research was fragmented. I reviewed 53 articles to map the antecedents, drivers, outcomes, theories, typology and methods used to study AR in online retailing. The result is a consolidated framework of consumer behaviour toward AR.
