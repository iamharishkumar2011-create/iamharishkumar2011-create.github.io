Services for consumers with disabilities

In One Line A framework for making services accessible at every stage, from planning to after-use.
Journal of Services Marketing (2023) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/JSM-06-2023-0204 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"Reviewing 77 articles, the study proposes a framework with four dimensions: going beyond regulations toward accessibility, value co-creation, inclusion of consumers with disabilities, and the role of stakeholders across planning, usage, and post-usage service stages."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, Journal of Services Marketing (2023)

Plain-English Summary

Research on serving consumers with disabilities was scattered across disciplines. We reviewed 77 articles following the PRISMA protocol and organised findings by service design stage: planning, usage and post-usage. We then proposed a framework and a research agenda based on the Double Diamond design model.
