Crafting inclusive marketing campaigns in India

In One Line What makes LGBTQIA+-inclusive ads feel authentic to Indian consumers.
Australasian Marketing Journal (2024) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/14413582241247328 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"Interviews with 29 consumers identified seven factors behind effective inclusive campaigns: social acceptance of the community, community-specific symbols, authentic communication about community issues, top management's approach, inclusive hiring, brand history, and regulatory/legal landscape alignment."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, Australasian Marketing Journal (2024)

Plain-English Summary

Brands increasingly run inclusive campaigns, but managers had little evidence on what makes them work. We first interviewed 29 LGBTQIA+ and non-LGBTQ consumers in India, then tested the factors in a second, quantitative phase.

Key Statistics & Metrics

29 In-Depth Consumer Interviews Qualitative Phase

For Managers & Industry Leaders

💡
Strategic Implication: Authentic campaigns require internal alignment: consumer trust requires inclusive hiring and management commitment to accompany external ad messaging.
