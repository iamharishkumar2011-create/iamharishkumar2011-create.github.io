Augmented reality as a brand advocate

In One Line AR experiences empower users, which builds engagement and brand advocacy.
Journal of Consumer Behaviour (2023) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/cb.2227 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"In a survey of 502 AR users analysed with structural equation modelling, AR empowered users while shopping, which built brand attachment and engagement and, in turn, brand advocacy."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, Journal of Consumer Behaviour (2023)

Plain-English Summary

Research showed AR shifts attitudes and purchase intentions, but not whether it builds deeper bonds with brands. We surveyed AR users to test whether and how AR turns them into brand advocates, looking beyond AR's technical features to its effect on brands.

Key Statistics & Metrics

N = 502 Verified AR Shoppers Survey Sample
Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) Methodology
Empowerment → Attachment & Engagement → Advocacy Causal Mechanism

For Managers & Industry Leaders

💡
Strategic Implication: Empowering consumers with spatial control converts passive shoppers into enthusiastic brand advocates who generate organic word-of-mouth.
