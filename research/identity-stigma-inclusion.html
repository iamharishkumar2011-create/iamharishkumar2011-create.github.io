Identity stigmatization and consumer inclusion

In One Line How inclusive advertising campaigns can dismantle identity-based stigma in the marketplace.
International Journal of Advertising (2025) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/02650487.2025.2592469 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"Based on 24 in-depth interviews, inclusive campaigns reduce identity stigma in five ways: giving LGBTQIA+ consumers visibility, legitimising their status as consumers, sensitising heterosexual cisgender consumers, acting as proof-of-value for inclusion, and creating a safe space."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, International Journal of Advertising (2025)

Plain-English Summary

LGBTQIA+ consumers in India face identity-based stigma in the marketplace. Through in-depth interviews, we traced how that stigma works: these identities are segregated, discriminated against and denied basic resources. We then examined how LGBTQ-inclusive campaigns can counter it.
