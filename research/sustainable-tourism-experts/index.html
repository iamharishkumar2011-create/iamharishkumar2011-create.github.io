Sustainable tourism practices

In One Line Identifying the critical industry components for sustainable tourism via international expert consensus.
Environmental Science and Pollution Research (2024) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11356-023-31503-7 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"Interviews with 26 tourism experts from eight countries identify the components most critical to advancing sustainable tourism using interval type-2 fuzzy set analysis."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, Environmental Science and Pollution Research (2024)

Plain-English Summary

Making tourism sustainable needs insight from the people who run the industry. We interviewed tourism experts across eight countries and analysed their views with interval type-2 fuzzy sets to find which components matter most.

Key Statistics & Metrics

26 Industry & Academic Tourism Experts Expert Panel
8 Nations Represented Geographic Diversity
Interval Type-2 Fuzzy Sets Analytical Method
