Inclusive marketing: review and research agenda

In One Line A refined definition of inclusive marketing and a map of where the field stands.
Journal of Business Research (2025) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2025.115274 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"Reviewing 71 articles, the study refines the definition of inclusive marketing as a human-centred, value-driven and integrated marketing approach that intentionally designs and delivers offerings, communications, and experiences to acknowledge, respect, and empower diverse consumer identities."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, Journal of Business Research (2025)

Plain-English Summary

Diverse, multicultural markets have made inclusive marketing a fast-growing topic, but its definition and how consumers respond to it were unclear. We reviewed 71 articles using the TCCM framework (theories, contexts, characteristics, methods). The review sorts the field into six streams, from representation of diverse consumers to critical views of inclusive marketing.
