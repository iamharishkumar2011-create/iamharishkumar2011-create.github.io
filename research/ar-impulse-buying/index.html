AR and online impulse buying

In One Line How real-time AR try-on triggers online impulse buying for high body-involvement products.
International Journal of Retail & Distribution Management (2022) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/IJRDM-11-2021-0535 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"AR virtual try-on significantly increases online impulse behaviour by adding hedonic value and lowering perceived product risk before purchase."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, International Journal of Retail & Distribution Management (2022)

Plain-English Summary

Online shoppers can't touch or try on high body-involvement products such as clothing or cosmetics. We tested whether AR virtual try-on drives online impulse buying for these products, and whether flow and spatial presence explain the effect.

Key Statistics & Metrics

High Body-Involvement Goods (Fashion & Cosmetics) Product Category
Flow State & Spatial Presence Psychological Drivers
Reduced Pre-Purchase Perceived Risk Direct Benefit

For Managers & Industry Leaders

💡
Strategic Implication: Integrating accurate virtual try-on eliminates hesitation for high-involvement fashion and personal goods, driving spontaneous conversions.
