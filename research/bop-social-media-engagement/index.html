Social media engagement at the bottom of the pyramid

In One Line Uncovering how vulnerable BoP consumers balance resource acquisition and security fears on social media.
Journal of International Consumer Marketing (2024) DOI: https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RQaX1WwAAAAJ ↗
🏆
Honour / Award Best Paper Award at the Great Lakes Winter Business School Conference (December 2022)
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"From 26 in-depth interviews, BoP users engage both positively and negatively: they treat social media as a resource and judge its fit with their abilities and social relevance, while security fears and restrictions hold them back."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, Journal of International Consumer Marketing (2024)

Plain-English Summary

Bottom-of-the-pyramid (BoP) consumers are a fast-growing and vulnerable group on social media. Using grounded theory, we explored how and why Indian BoP users engage with social media platforms, and how that differs from other segments.
