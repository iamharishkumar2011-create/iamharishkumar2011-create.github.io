Plain-English Summary

Firms rely on digital technologies for engagement and sales, yet managers still lack a clear picture of how users engage with them. We reviewed the research on digital customer engagement, covering its theories, methods, contexts, antecedents, drivers and outcomes, plus the leading authors, journals and countries.
