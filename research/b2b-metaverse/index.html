B2B marketing in the metaverse

In One Line A conceptual framework demonstrating how immersive 3D environments transform industrial B2B commerce.
Journal of Business & Industrial Marketing (2026) DOI: https://doi.org/10.1108/JBIM-09-2024-0679 ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"A three-part framework (innovation and collaboration ecosystems, advanced marketing initiatives, and augmented service and support) shows how immersive, multisensory metaverse environments improve B2B service, engagement, personalisation, collaboration and innovation, and speed up decision-making."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, Journal of Business & Industrial Marketing (2026)

Plain-English Summary

B2B firms are starting to use the metaverse, but research on how it changes business marketing was in its infancy. This conceptual paper builds a theoretical framework for B2B marketing in the metaverse from the existing literature. It also gives managers a roadmap for adopting metaverse technologies.
