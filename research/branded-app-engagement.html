Branded app engagement: grounded theory

In One Line Mapping the three triggers and mechanisms behind consumer branded app engagement.
Asia Pacific Journal of Information Systems (2021) DOI: https://doi.org/10.14329/apjis.2021.31.4.582 ↗
🔓 Open Access Repository Version:
Free to Read (Open access on KoreaScience) ↗
Key Finding (Quotable Scientific Claim)
"From 24 interviews, branded app engagement has three triggers: media characteristics (privacy and security, customization, convenience), brand aspects (brand image, product or service quality) and benefits sought (inherent and derived)."
— Dr. Harish Kumar, Asia Pacific Journal of Information Systems (2021)

Plain-English Summary

Brands invest heavily in mobile apps, but why consumers engage with them was poorly understood. Using grounded theory, we mapped what triggers branded app engagement and the mechanism behind each trigger.

Key Statistics & Metrics
