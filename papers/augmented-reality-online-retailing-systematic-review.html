Plain-English Summary

When augmented reality first migrated from aerospace labs to mobile e-commerce in the 2010s, academic research developed in an ad-hoc, fragmented fashion across disparate computer science and marketing journals. There was no unified map of what had been discovered.

In this sole-authored study, Dr. Harish Kumar conducted the definitive systematic review of the field's first decade. By rigorously analyzing more than 100 empirical and conceptual papers, the study synthesized the literature into five foundational pillars: interface technological attributes, consumer psychological states, behavioral outcomes, retailer implementation challenges, and emerging ethical concerns.

Cited extensively across global spatial computing research, this milestone paper provided the intellectual foundation and research agenda that shaped subsequent empirical breakthroughs in AR retailing.
