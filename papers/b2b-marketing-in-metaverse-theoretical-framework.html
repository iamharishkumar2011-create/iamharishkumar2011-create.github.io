Plain-English Summary

While popular discourse around the metaverse has focused heavily on consumer video games and virtual real estate hype, enterprise business-to-business (B2B) applications represent the most economically consequential frontier of spatial computing.

This study builds the first comprehensive theoretical architecture for how industrial enterprises conduct relational marketing within 3D immersive environments. The authors examine how multi-party buying centers leverage interactive digital twins—exact virtual replicas of industrial machinery, supply facilities, and complex infrastructure—to evaluate high-capital purchase decisions across international borders without physical travel.

The research establishes guidelines for inter-organizational governance, demonstrating how spatial presence bridges geographic friction, enhances mutual accountability, and fundamentally reconfigures relationship management in 21st-century manufacturing.
