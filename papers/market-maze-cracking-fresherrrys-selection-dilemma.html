Plain-English Summary

Delivering fresh farm produce in emerging economies is one of the world's most brutal operational challenges: fragile cold chains, perishable inventory spoilage exceeding 30%, fragmented smallholder farms, and entrenched middleman networks.

This case tracks the founders of 'Fresherrry', an innovative agritech startup, as they confront a life-or-death market expansion dilemma. Should they pour limited venture capital into direct-to-consumer app delivery in expensive metropolitan zones, or should they dominate high-volume B2B supply contracts with hotels and modern supermarket chains?

Students navigate raw unit economics, customer acquisition costs (CAC), perishable logistics, and channel conflict, learning how early-stage tech ventures must balance rapid headline growth with fundamental cash-flow sustainability.
