Plain-English Summary

Traditional online commerce forces shoppers into a passive role: scrolling through flat photos, reading bulleted descriptions, and hoping the real item matches expectations. When shoppers interact with products through spatial AR, that psychological dynamic flips entirely.

When consumers manipulate a virtual product in 3D—rotating it, scaling it inside their own room, or customizing colors—their brains begin experiencing 'psychological ownership'. Even before checking out, consumers subconsciously perceive the product as part of their personal identity and physical space.

This study empirically proves that this sense of spatial ownership transforms ordinary buyers into active brand ambassadors. Consumers who co-create their experience in AR are 47% more likely to capture screenshots, share them with friends, and recommend the brand organically.
