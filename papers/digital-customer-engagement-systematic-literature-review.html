Digital Customer Engagement: A Systematic Literature Review and Research Agenda

Plain-English Summary

In the modern digital economy, 'customer engagement' has become a ubiquitous corporate buzzword, frequently degraded into superficial vanity metrics like likes, retweets, and brief pageviews. This systematic literature review brings intellectual rigor back to engagement science.

By synthesizing over 120 seminal publications across marketing, management, and information systems, the researchers map how engagement actually unfolds as a tripartite, dynamic feedback system comprising cognitive focus, emotional affection, and behavioral participation.

The authors demonstrate why short-term promotional gimmicks fail: true commercial retention occurs when a digital system provides intellectual utility that reduces consumer effort. The paper establishes a research roadmap for the era of AI and algorithmically personalized interfaces.
