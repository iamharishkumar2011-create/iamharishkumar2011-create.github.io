From Margins to Mainstream: Identifying Successful LGBTQ+ Inclusive Marketing Campaigns in India

Authors: Upadhyay, S., Tuli, N., & Kumar, H.
Australasian Marketing Journal Vol. 32, No. 3, 215–229 DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/14413582241247328 ↗

Plain-English Summary

Following the historic 2018 Supreme Court of India ruling decriminalizing Section 377, corporate brands in the world's most populous nation rushed to express support for LGBTQ+ communities. However, brands quickly discovered that emerging markets present distinct cultural dynamics: clumsy executions triggered intense polarization and accusations of 'rainbow-washing'.

This research investigates the narrative architecture of twelve landmark Indian campaigns that successfully challenged social prejudice while preserving broad commercial appeal (including famous campaigns by Fastrack, Bhima Jewellers, and Urban Company).

The authors discover that the most resonant, durable campaigns grounded queer characters within universal Indian interpersonal ties—family acceptance, filial duty, weddings, and professional dignity. By making inclusion relatable rather than foreign or antagonistic, brands expanded cultural empathy and solidified market leadership.
