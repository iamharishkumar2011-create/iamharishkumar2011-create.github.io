Inclusive Marketing: A Review and Research Agenda

Authors: Tuli, N., Srivastava, V., & Kumar, H.
Journal of Business Research Vol. 186, 115274 DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2025.115274 ↗

Plain-English Summary

Historically, diversity and inclusion in commercial marketing were treated as peripheral public relations exercises—tokenized stock photography or brief cause-marketing campaigns during commemorative months. This research establishes that cosmetic representation without operational backing triggers intense consumer cynicism and damages brand equity.

By conducting a comprehensive systematic review of more than 180 academic papers, the authors develop a unified tripartite framework structuring inclusive marketing into institutional policies, brand narratives, and consumer experiences.

The paper provides executive leadership with concrete operational benchmarks: true inclusion requires universal product design, accessible retail architecture, dignified service interactions, and inclusive algorithmic systems. When executed authentically, inclusion transforms marketplace vulnerability into genuine commercial loyalty.
