Plain-English Summary

Consumers do not experience retail through isolated channels; they fluidly move between browsing on a sofa at midnight and walking through physical store aisles on Saturday afternoon. Augmented reality acts as the connective tissue linking these physical and digital spaces.

This research formulates an integrative taxonomy identifying four distinct modes of AR engagement: Exploratory (casual spatial discovery), Confirmatory (precise validation of size and aesthetic fit), Utilitarian (in-store wayfinding and ingredient scanning), and Experiential (immersive brand storytelling).

By mapping these modes across both in-store and remote e-commerce touchpoints, the authors equip retail executives with a comprehensive roadmap to design spatial applications that match the consumer's exact cognitive mindset at each point in their shopping journey.
