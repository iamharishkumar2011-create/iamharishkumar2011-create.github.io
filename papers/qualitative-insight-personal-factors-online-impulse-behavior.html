A Qualitative Insight into the Personal Factors Impacting Online Impulse Behavior

Authors: Kumar, H., Garg, R., Kumar, P., & Chhikara, R.

Plain-English Summary

Late-night smartphone shopping has become a pervasive fixture of modern youth culture. While consumers intend to simply browse social apps before sleep, they repeatedly find themselves completing unplanned digital checkouts for clothing, gadgets, and lifestyle goods.

This qualitative investigation probes into the psychological mechanisms behind this unplanned buying behavior. Conducting in-depth laddering interviews with young digital natives, the authors untangle how nocturnal fatigue diminishes executive self-regulation ('ego depletion').

When reduced willpower intersects with targeted algorithmic recommendations and fear of missing out (FOMO) from social media feeds, digital purchases become an emotional coping mechanism for stress relief and social validation. The chapter outlines consumer self-regulation strategies and responsible e-commerce design principles.
