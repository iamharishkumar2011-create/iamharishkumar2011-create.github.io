Plain-English Summary

Impulse buying online has historically been constrained by consumer doubt: 'Will this lamp look right in my living room?' or 'Will this watch look oversized on my wrist?' These cognitive hesitations pause the purchase impulse, leading to cart abandonment.

Through a controlled 2x2 experimental simulation with 340 active shoppers, this research measures what happens when augmented reality eliminates that hesitation. When shoppers project true-scale 3D models into their personal spaces, their cognitive friction evaporates in real time.

The study proves that AR elevates spontaneous buying behavior by 34%. By triggering rapid positive emotional arousal and removing spatial uncertainty, AR creates an experiential shortcut directly from inspiration to transaction.
