Plain-English Summary

More than one billion people worldwide live with disabilities, representing a consumer market larger than China. Yet, commercial retail and service operations continue to design spaces, websites, and service interactions as though disabled individuals do not exist.

This paper proposes an inclusive service ecosystem framework built around Transformative Service Research (TSR). The authors dissect the three primary layers of structural exclusion: architectural/physical barriers, informational/digital barriers, and interpersonal biases during frontline employee encounters.

The study proves that accessible service design is not charity or compliance—it is an enormous commercial growth engine. By eliminating barriers, brands restore basic human dignity to underserved customers while simultaneously improving convenience, clarity, and safety for all shoppers.
